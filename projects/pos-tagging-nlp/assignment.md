Lab 05: Part-of-Speech Tagging in the Real World
# 📋 Lab Overview
Welcome to the lab for Module 05! This lab dives into the world of Part-of-Speech (POS) tagging—the fundamental process of assigning grammatical roles (like noun, verb, adjective) to words in a sentence. You will move from foundational concepts to tackling the complexities of real-world text, such as messy social media posts and customer service transcripts.

This assignment requires you to actively write code in designated sections. The goal is to use coding as a tool to explore concepts and build your analytical skills.

Duration: This lab includes an in-class portion and a more extensive homework section, likely requiring 3-4 hours to complete thoughtfully.

## ⭐ Why This Lab Matters
Part-of-Speech tagging is a cornerstone of Natural Language Processing that operates silently in many applications you use daily. It's the "grammar police" that helps systems differentiate between "Apple stock" and "apple pie," powers Siri's command interpretation, and improves your phone's autocorrect. Mastering this technique is essential for building more advanced NLP systems for sentiment analysis, information extraction, and machine translation.

 

## 🎓 Learning Objectives
By completing this lab, you will be able to:

Understand and Apply the fundamentals of POS tagging using core NLP libraries (NLTK and SpaCy).

Compare and Contrast different tagging systems, including the Penn Treebank and Universal Dependencies tag sets.

Analyze and Handle ambiguous words and the challenges posed by messy, informal text from real-world sources.

Apply POS tagging to solve a practical business problem by analyzing customer service call transcripts for sentiment and urgency.

Benchmark and Evaluate the performance (speed and accuracy) of different POS taggers on various text types.

Create meaningful data visualizations to summarize your analysis.
