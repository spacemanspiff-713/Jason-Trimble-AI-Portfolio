Featured Project

KidMood: Classroom Emotion Recognition

ITAI 1378 — Computer Vision & AI Capstone

Problem & Approach

Teachers cannot continuously monitor every student's emotional state. KidMood uses computer vision to classify facial expressions as an assistive awareness tool.

  • FER-2013 facial expression dataset
  • MobileNetV2 transfer learning
  • 4-class classification: Happy, Sad, Angry, Fear
  • Stratified train/validation/test split
  • Baseline CNN for comparison
