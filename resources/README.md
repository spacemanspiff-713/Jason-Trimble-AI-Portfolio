# Annotated Resources

Key references and learning materials from ITAI 2372 and related coursework.

---

## AI in Finance

- **JPMorgan Chase using advanced AI to detect fraud** — *American Banker*  
  Primary source for the A04 fraud detection case study. Covers LLMs, entity extraction, and behavioral analytics in financial fraud prevention.

- **Artificial Intelligence in Finance: A Comprehensive Review** — Bahoo et al. (2024), *SN Business & Economics*  
  Academic survey of AI applications across finance including fraud detection, credit risk, and market surveillance.

---

## AI in Entertainment & Media

- **How AI is bringing film stars back from the dead** — *BBC Future* (Velasquez, 2023)  
  Examines digital clones of deceased actors and the ethical implications for consent and identity.

- **Deepfakes of your dead loved ones are a booming Chinese business** — *MIT Technology Review* (2024)  
  Reports on grief technology using AI to create digital replicas of deceased family members.

- **Music Industry and Generative AI** — Course resource  
  Overview of AI music creation tools (AIVA, Suno, Udio) and their impact on artists and the industry.

## AI in Smart Cities

  Real-world examples of AI deployment in urban infrastructure, transportation, and public services.

- **Artificial Intelligence in the Smart City: A Literature Review** — Academic paper  
  Comprehensive review of AI applications in smart city contexts.
